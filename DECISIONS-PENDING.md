# Decisions pending — not blockers

These are conflicts or absences inside the specification. The build continued
with the more specific rule, or with the rule that produces more honest evidence.

1. **Slack.** The P2 deliverable line says “0.5 h slack”. §7.5’s table says 10% of the weekly budget stays unallocated (60 minutes at 10 h). The table is what the allocator enforces.
2. **Activation and E1a.** §7.2 says a successful activation restores E3. §7.1 says E1a never counts toward a gate. A successful activation restores E3 only if the capability had already reached E2 or E3. E1a stays E1a.
3. **R4 and R5 inputs.** The exclusion rules are implemented (no questions, below 95%, or already-studied material never enter). The texts and the unannounced listening ladder are P3 content. Until they exist the indicators stay unmeasured. A self-report is not accepted as a number.
4. **Human pronunciation recordings are still missing.** The timed chunk drill and the reading texts exist. Listening is device speech, labelled as such. Dialect audio is not faked. A bundled human reference for the A0–A1 core (decision D2) is not recorded.
5. **Lesson prerequisites versus gates.** A lesson prerequisite is met by unassisted E1 or higher. E1a does not satisfy it. Gates still open only on E3. Requiring E3 before the next A0 lesson would freeze the learner before any pressure evidence can exist.
6. **Official lists are not ingested.** The learner asked for every unit now, so the bodies were written on the mapped sequence. That is not a line-by-line check against the Goethe Wortlisten. Coverage of those lists is not claimed.
7. **Mock papers are original, not the official five-part and four-part forms.** A timed 30-item paper produces a module score out of 100. It is not a Goethe paper and does not claim the official part structure. Protocol mocks do not count before teaching month 26.


8. **The opening lesson carries no word list yet.** `a0-u1-l1` is hand-written (30 steps, 24 capability ids) and predates the lexical layer. It is a declared exception in `tools/validate-lesson.js`; the validator prints it on every run. Porting it means rewriting it as a spec like its five neighbours.
9. **A0 and A1 are closed; A2–B2 are mid-port.** The lexical layer exists for A0 (complete, one declared exception) and for A1 (twenty-four lessons, 576 of 644 declared items, DELIVERED at 89%). `PENDING_VOCAB_LEVELS` in the validator names A2, B1 and B2 only: A1 left the list this round, so from now on an A1 lesson without a word list fails the build instead of printing a backlog line. The `vocab-a1.js` file is a slice file like `vocab-a0a1.js`; the next slice is `vocab-a2.js`.
9a. **The coverage gate now checks two promises, not one.** The first rule was "a level that starts the lexical layer must reach 80% of its own declaration". That rule made honest incremental porting impossible: the first ported lesson of A1 would have turned the build red, or forced a false `delivered` claim. The gate is now (1) per lesson — a ported lesson carries ≥80% of **its own map row**, which is stricter than before because a thin lesson can no longer hide inside a fat level — and (2) per level — a level whose rows are all ported (a declared exception counting as ported) is claimed as DELIVERED and must hold 80% of its level declaration. Anything else prints `mid-port (not claimed)`. A0 is DELIVERED at 83% with its one declared exception; A1 is DELIVERED at 89% with all 24 rows ported. The exception `a0-u1-l1` is declared in two places on purpose (`validate-lesson.js` and `validate-syllabus.js`) and printed by both.
10. **Words are authored, not extracted from the Goethe lists.** The lists are still not ingested (see item 6). The word list is written from the mapped sequence, so coverage of the official Wortlisten is not claimed.
11. **A lesson's word list now sets the step budget.** §8.3 caps one Wortschatz step at four words, and §19 caps a lesson at 36 steps. The A1 rows declare 28 words, which five Wortschatz steps cannot carry and still obey §8.3. The compiler therefore sizes the number of Wortschatz steps from the list (24 items → six steps of four, inside 32 steps total); the step bound in `validate-lesson.js` moved from [3,5] to [3,7] for that reason. The map declaration is the promise and the structure follows it, not the reverse.
12. **The step-card lookup is qualified by lesson.** `DW.BANK.STEP_CARDS` is keyed by the bare step id and belongs to the hand-written opening lesson; every lesson has an `s12`. Reading that bank first meant the opening lesson's greetings were introduced as review cards by every other lesson, and the lesson's own words never reached the queue. The qualified catalogue (`lessonId:stepId`) is now read first, and the bank is consulted only for `a0-u1-l1`. `tools/p3-unit.mjs` pins it: step `s12` of `a0-u1-l6` queues `antworten`, never `Danke`. Card counters before this fix measured the opening lesson's greetings, not the lessons' words.
13. **The agreed visual direction (dirB) is implemented, but in the product's own language.** The reference screen was drawn with German labels; §9 fixes the UI language as Arabic with German content, and that rule wins: the direction was taken as a visual system (dark surfaces, one decision per screen, hero card, bottom navigation, inline SVG icons), not as a language change. If the learner ever wants a German UI, it is a settings change (`settings.uiLanguage` already exists with `ar`), not a redesign.
14. **The five tabs are not the five tabs of a course app.** `Lernen` became **الخريطة** (capability map), not a lesson browser, because a browsable course is an anti-goal (§18) and the tool — not the learner — picks the lesson. `Community` was not built at all: it needs accounts and a server, both anti-goals. Its slot is taken by **المؤشرات**, which the learner actually needs daily. The Training tab collects the instruments that used to be fourteen identical grey buttons on the home screen.
15. **The service worker cache name is now part of every UI release.** A cache-first asset list means an installed client would keep an old interface after a redesign. `sw.js` moves to a new `deutschweg-vNN` on each UI round; this round is `deutschweg-v10`.
16. **Word-order scoring reads the declared Vorfeld, and field attribution counts occurrences.** Level 1 used to be `finIdx === 1`, i.e. "the finite verb is the second token". A Vorfeld is a constituent: in "Das Buch liegt auf dem Tisch" it is two tokens, so eight of the thirty-four shipped items scored a correct sentence as zero and logged a false error. The check now uses `fields.vorfeld`, with the single-token case as the fallback for items that declare no fields. The same round fixed a second defect: fields were attributed to tokens by text, so a repeated token ("Ich möchte einen Kaffee. Ich will schlafen") borrowed the other occurrence's field and broke a correct sentence. Attribution is now positional, by occurrence number, in the scorer and in the field colouring. `tools/p1-unit.mjs` pins both cases; `tools/p3-unit.mjs` runs every shipped item through the engine's own scorer.
17. **The Perfekt gets two lessons, not one, and the reason is the learner's real error.** Separating `haben` (a1-u3-l4) from `sein` (a1-u3-l5) costs a lesson but matches the error the Arabic-speaking learner actually makes: the frame `ge…t` is built correctly by rule, while `sein` with movement or change of state is chosen by meaning and is the one place where a correct-looking `habe gegangen` appears. The wrong form is written into the lesson's error list on purpose so it is read once, named, and never silently tolerated. The `nach Hause` / `zu Hause` pair sits in the same lesson for the same reason: direction and location are one decision, and German marks it with two prepositions the learner hears as one.
18. **A1 is claimed as DELIVERED at 89%, and the 68 missing items are declared, not averaged away.** Every A1 row is ported and every lesson carries 24 of its row's 28 declared items (86%); the level total is 576 of 644 (89%). The gate reads delivery as 80% plus every row ported, so the claim is allowed. It is worth writing down what it does not mean: the four items per row that are not authored are not hidden in a fat lesson, they are the same four everywhere, and the vocabulary is written from the mapped sequence, not extracted from a Goethe list (item 6). Those two sentences must travel with the word DELIVERED wherever it is shown.
19. **The exam-form lesson teaches the paper, not the score.** `a1-u4-l6` carries the words of the exam sheet (Teil, Lücke, ankreuzen, zuordnen, buchstabieren, Reihenfolge) and three facts that change how the learner uses the room: no part compensates another, listening is heard a fixed number of times, and spelling is a scored part of speaking. No mock paper, no score estimate, and no readiness claim is made at A1 — the module engine stays where it was (P4, P3 content).
